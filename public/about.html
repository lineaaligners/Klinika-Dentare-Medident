Klinika Dentare Medident — Dental Clinic in Pejë, Kosovo

Klinika Dentare Medident is a family dental clinic in Pejë (Peja), Kosovo, founded in 1999 by Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani. What started as one dentist in one room has grown into a 200 m² full-service dental centre with 15 staff members and 13,000+ patients treated — without ever losing the feel of a clinic run by people who know your name.

Our team

  • Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani — Oral Surgery Specialist and Implantologist. Founder. First woman in Kosovo to place a dental implant (2004). 27 years of experience, 4,000+ implants placed. Educated at Çapa University, Istanbul.
