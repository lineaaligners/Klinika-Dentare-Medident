Frequently asked questions

What is included?

The treatment at a fixed price and, on request, arranging your transfer and accommodation. You book your own flights.

How long do I need to stay?

Crowns and veneers: one stay of 5–7 days. Implants: two stays (3–5 and 5–7 days) about 3–4 months apart.
