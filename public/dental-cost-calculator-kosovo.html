How we calculate

Medident prices are fixed: implant with zirconia crown €600, zirconia crown €150, porcelain veneer €250, bone graft €200. Prices for the UK, Ireland and Sweden are approximate private-clinic prices (2026) and vary by clinic. Flights and accommodation are not included – see dental holiday packages.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result binding?

No, it is an estimate. You get the binding price in writing in your 3D plan after your X-ray and consultation – free of charge.

What does the 3D plan show?

An animated film of each treatment, your tooth chart, visits and costs. In the calculator the tooth positions are examples; your real plan is made by Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani.

Send your X-ray – plan and quote within 24 hours

Send us your panoramic X-ray (OPG) or CBCT. Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani reviews it and you get a free provisional treatment plan with price and length of stay – in English.
