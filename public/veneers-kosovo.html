Frequently asked questions

Are veneers right for everyone?

Not always. With strong misalignment, aligners (e.g. Linea) or crowns may be better. We decide this at the consultation.

How many veneers do I need?

Usually 6–10 for the teeth that show when you smile. A photo of your smile is enough for a first assessment.
