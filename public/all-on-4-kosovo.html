What is All-on-4?

All-on-4 (or All-on-6) anchors a fixed bridge on four to six implants per jaw. The back implants are angled, so a bone graft is often not needed. It is the most common solution for loose teeth or full dentures.

Process and trips

  1. From home: send your X-ray (OPG/CBCT) or photos – get a free plan, price and length of stay.
  2. Trip 1 (5–7 days): CBCT, 3D planning, extractions, implants and usually a fixed temporary bridge (immediate loading).
  3. Healing (3–6 months) at home with fixed temporary teeth.
  4. Trip 2 (5–7 days): final zirconia bridge.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do I need for All-on-4?

Usually two stays of 5–7 days: first the implants with a fixed temporary bridge, then after 3–6 months the final zirconia bridge.

Do I need a bone graft?

Often not, because the back implants are angled. Your CBCT shows whether you need one.

Will I have teeth in between?

In suitable cases yes: you leave after the first trip with fixed temporary teeth.
