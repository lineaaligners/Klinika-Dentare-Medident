Dental Implants in Pejë, Kosovo

Klinika Dentare Medident offers dental implant surgery in Pejë, Kosovo, led by Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani — oral surgery specialist, implantologist, and the first woman in Kosovo to place a dental implant, back in 2004. She still reviews every implant case personally before a patient commits to anything.

What implant systems does Medident use?

  • Hiossen ET III (USA) — FDA-cleared, SA Surface treatment, tapered design, high primary stability
  • MegaGen AnyRidge (South Korea) — CE certified, XPEED Surface, KnifeThread design

Our approach: 3D-planned guided surgery

Every implant case at Medident begins with a CBCT 3D scan on our Sirona Orthophos SL unit. Dr. Lendita plans the exact angle, depth, and diameter of each implant digitally before surgery. A 3D-printed surgical guide is made for that patient, so the implant is placed through a precise, pre-planned path — flapless when possible. Shorter surgery, less swelling, faster recovery.

Experience

How long does implant treatment take?

A single implant is typically placed in one short surgical visit, guided by a 3D-printed stent planned from your CBCT scan. Full healing before the final crown usually takes 2-4 months; immediate loading with a temporary crown is available in suitable cases.

Does it hurt?

Guided surgery is planned digitally in advance, which allows flapless placement in most cases — a smaller incision, less swelling, and a faster recovery than traditional freehand surgery.
