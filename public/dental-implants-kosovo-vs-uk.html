How your implant trip works

  1. From home: send your X-ray (OPG) or a photo – get a free plan, price and length of stay.
  2. Trip 1 (3–5 days): CBCT, 3D planning and implant placement. Fly into Pristina or Podgorica; transfer to Pejë.
  3. Healing (about 3–4 months) at home: the implant fuses with the bone. We stay in touch on WhatsApp.
  4. Trip 2 (5–7 days): digital impression with Primescan and fitting of the zirconia crown.

In suitable cases immediate loading is possible – you leave trip 1 with a fixed temporary tooth.
