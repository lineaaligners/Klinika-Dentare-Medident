Frequently asked questions

How much does a dental implant cost in Kosovo?

At Medident in Pejë an implant (MegaGen BlueDiamond or Hiossen) costs €450 including the abutment, or from €600 with a zirconia crown (€150). Bone grafting (€200) or extraction (€30) only when needed.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. You can also send an X-ray (OPG) on WhatsApp beforehand and receive a preliminary plan and price before you travel.

How many trips do I need?

Usually two for implants: placement, then the final crown after 2–4 months of healing. Crowns and veneers are typically done in 5–7 days.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, instalment payment is available. We explain the terms at the consultation.
