Frequently asked questions

Is dental treatment in Kosovo safe?

Yes, if you choose an experienced clinic. Medident was founded in 1999, has treated over 13,000 patients and placed over 4,000 implants. We use MegaGen BlueDiamond implants, Sirona CBCT and Primescan 2, and sterilise to European standards.

Who will treat me?

Implants and surgery are performed by Dr. Lendita Islami Nallbani, oral surgeon with 27 years of experience. Crowns and prosthetics are done by our team, including Dr. Faton Loci.

How many trips do I need for dental implants?

Usually two: trip 1 (3–5 days) for implant placement, then after about 3–4 months of healing, trip 2 (5–7 days) for the crowns. With immediate loading you leave trip 1 with a fixed temporary tooth.

How long do crowns or veneers take?

Zirconia crowns and veneers are normally finished in 5–7 days – one stay.

What if I have a problem after I get home?

You can reach us directly on WhatsApp. We review photos and X-rays and, if needed, arrange a check-up. Aftercare terms are given to you in writing with your plan.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You are looked after in English before your trip on WhatsApp and at the clinic, and your 3D treatment plan is in English.

How do I get to Pejë?

The nearest airports are Pristina (about 1 h 15 min) and Podgorica, Montenegro (about 2 h 30 min). We help arrange transfer and accommodation.

How much is the consultation?

The consultation is free. Send us an X-ray (OPG) or photo first and you receive a provisional plan with price before you book.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, instalments are possible. We discuss the terms at the consultation.

Do I need an X-ray from home?

Not necessarily. An OPG or CBCT speeds up planning, but a photo of your teeth is enough to start. We take our own 3D scan (Sirona CBCT) at the clinic.

What can I do in Pejë between appointments?

Visit the Rugova Canyon, the via ferrata and zip line, and Pejë old town – a dental holiday in the mountains.
